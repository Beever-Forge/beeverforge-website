Frequently asked questions

Choose with clarity.

Will Community remain open source?

Yes. The OpenFactory core behind BeeverForge Community is available under Apache-2.0. You can run it on your own infrastructure. Models, machines and external services may have separate costs.

Is Enterprise available now?

The expanded workflow and Prototype Builder are in development. Availability of SSO, RBAC and other commercial details still needs confirmation. This page describes the product direction, not a demo of released modules.

Does Community include a PO?

The core documents an optional Product Owner requirements workflow. In the Enterprise journey, PO means Product Owner; the precise role and handoff between new modules still need confirmation.

Who decides what reaches production?

In the documented core, policies govern execution steps and promotion to production requires human approval. Independent review is advisory by default.

How much does it cost?

Community code is Apache-2.0, while agents and infrastructure may incur costs. Enterprise is a paid edition with pricing on request.
